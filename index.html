Project

AttendX

The Problem

Manual attendance and scattered academic workflows are inefficient — paper registers, spreadsheets, and no single place for students to track their own academic standing.

The Idea

A centralized academic management application that brings attendance and academic workflows into one role-based app.

The Solution

AttendX combines admin management, teacher tools and student tools — attendance, location verification, rotating temporary codes, assignments and academic tracking, all in one place.
